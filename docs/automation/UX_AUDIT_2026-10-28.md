Barrier fade interpolates the `"x,y"` map key. **Summon AI turns dump `[decide]` / `[cast]` / `(x,y)` / intern ids (`UX-SUMMON-AI-LOG-DUMP`).** **Apply-effect lines print `e-3` (`UX-EFFECT-APPLY-TARGETID`).** **Defeat logs “Returning to map” while the next beat is the Death Realm (`UX-DEFEAT-RETURNING-MAP`).** **Void Rift warp prints `(x,y)` (`UX-VOID-RIFT-COORD`).** **Leader death prefixes `[Leader died]` (`UX-LEADER-DIED-BRACKET`).** **Mid-fight spell-bar queue uses two toast sentences (`UX-SPELLBAR-DEFER-TOAST`).**
